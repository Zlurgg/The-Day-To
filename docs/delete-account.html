Delete Your Data

No Account Required

The Day To does not use accounts. There is no sign-in, and none of your data is stored on any server. Everything lives only on your device, so deleting it is entirely in your hands.

Delete Individual Entries

You can remove specific journal entries or custom mood colors within the app:

  • Journal entry: open the entry and tap "Delete entry"
  • Mood color: open "Manage Mood Colors" from the menu and delete it

Delete All Your Data

To permanently remove all of your journal entries, mood colors, and settings:

1
Open Android Settings Go to Apps and select The Day To
2
Clear storage Tap Storage & cache, then Clear storage (or Clear data)
3
Or uninstall Uninstalling the app also deletes all of its data from your device
Warning:

This is permanent and cannot be undone. Because your data is never stored anywhere else, it cannot be recovered afterwards.
